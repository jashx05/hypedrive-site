HypeDrive

HypeDrive is a personal application used by its owner to organize monthly HYPE transaction exports and maintain an annual expense report in Google Drive.

What the application does

The workflow reads transaction data through a read-only banking connection, creates monthly CSV reports, and stores those reports in a Google Drive folder selected by the owner.

HypeDrive does not initiate payments, transfer money, sell financial services, or make the owner's private financial data publicly available.

Google Drive access

Google Drive access is used only to create, read, update, and organize files needed for the owner's personal expense-reporting workflow.
